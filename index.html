Frequently Asked Questions

Visit the Paper Alibi Etsy store and purchase the game. You'll receive a PDF file with instructions on how to play as well as all the evidence you'll need to solve the case. Following the link or QR code in the PDF file will take you to the online part of the game.

Most of the puzzles in the game are logic puzzles that are often hidden in plain sight. They're the kind of puzzles you might find at an escape room, such as forming a sentence out of all the underlined words on a piece of evidence.


Some of the puzzles are more thematic and relate directly to the story. For example, creating a timeline of events to rule out certain suspects.

You'll need a copy of the game, which you can purchase from the Paper Alibi Etsy store. It's recommended you print the PDF file out, but it's not required. You'll also need an internet connection so you can access the online part of the game. You're encouraged to take notes with a pen and paper, and some puzzles might benefit from a calculator.

Yes, the game comes with an online hint system. Don't be afraid to take hints when you need them. A solution is also available for each puzzle if you get completely stuck, though we encourage you to try solving it yourself first.

The approximate duration of our first game is between 1.5 and 2 hours. This may vary depending on your skill set, how many people are playing, and how many hints you choose to take.
